# Sound

Off by default; it starts only from the Sound button. Quiet plucked strings: triangle plus a soft octave, a low-pass sweep, about 1.6 s of decay.

- Each app is a silk string: PCC D (293.66 Hz), music A (220 Hz), chess E (329.63 Hz). A receipt plucks its app's string once, as its packet lands.
- Recall ends in a soft low tone at the hub (D 146.83 Hz with a quieter A above).
- The mic opening sounds a rising pluck (A then E); closing, a falling one.
- A confirmation sounds two soft plucks (G 392 Hz) once, never again while it waits.
- Queued and running steps make no sound. Nothing loops.
- A new app gets its own string in the same register, a consonant interval from the others.
