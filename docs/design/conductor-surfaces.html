Round 3: the surfaces

  • Live plan view. Each step’s arrow opens its plan body and its event trail (planned, started, receipt, paused, resumed, ended). A {s1} stays a dashed ref until step 1’s receipt fills it with the real title.
  • Question card (S17). This is conductor’s own ask_user, so it’s blue; an app’s confirmation stays amber. Tap, type or say an option. The answer resumes the same plan as {"resume": …}.
  • Proposal card (S14). A think step runs in the background on the bigger model and comes back as a proposal: Accept, Change (an amend on the pending plan), or Not now.
  • Jobs tray. Every running job and background think gets one line, with Cancel and Change. Try “Two jobs” and “Change a job”.
  • Timeline tab. One timeline across every surface, with day separators and search. Side threads carry their own small context, and their result flows back when you close one. Incognito turns keep their receipts but not their words.
  • Memory tab. Each fact says where it came from. Edit and Forget are recorded as your own note turns.
  • Voice only. The panel goes away, the screen fades, and captions say what you’d hear. The mic answers whatever is waiting: a question, a confirmation, or a proposal.

Real shapes

  • Plans are #70’s compiled plans: steps over the ten kinds, a direct {answer}, or {resume} for an ask_user answer. remember and forget (S20, S22) are kinds this prototype proposes; the step says so.
  • Receipts are delegate v1.1 record entries. Conductor’s own steps carry source: conductor, as in ledger_receipts.
  • Jobs use the A2A words and done / total, as in ledger_jobs.
  • Proposed here: a proposal {id, title, model, took, inputs[], items[[when, kind, what]], changed}, a memory fact {id, text, src}, and a side thread {id, title, carries[], closed, result}.

Gaps for the backend

  • #78: a think result has no shape and no home. The proposal card needs the fields above, a row it’s kept in, and an event while it runs, so the tray can show it.
  • #74: the delegate contract has jobs/{ref}/cancel but no amend. Changing a running job needs an app endpoint, and a receipt with the new total.
  • #89: ask_user options name titles only. To fill {s1} with a ref, not just a title, each option needs its (app, kind, ref).
  • #81: there’s no step kind for remember or forget, and the ten kinds are gated by the plan evals. The facts need {text, source turn, said at}.
  • #82: ledger_turns needs incognito and thread_id, plus a threads table. Recall and the state block must skip incognito words but keep their receipts. Search runs over words and receipt titles.
