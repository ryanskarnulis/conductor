A strand means a call to another app; work at the hub is conductor’s own. Only a receipt event draws the last 20%. The scene and the panel are pure functions of the same ledger events.

Accessibility

  • The panel is DOM and carries every status as a word. The 3D scene is aria-hidden decoration.
  • One polite live region announces what was heard, the plan, receipts, confirmations, failures and the finished note.
  • Reduced motion gives a still scene. The canvas renders only when the ledger changes.

Budgets, measured here
